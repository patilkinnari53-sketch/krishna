Important Tour Guidelines

  • Camera & Photography: Mobile photography is allowed, but Deity photos are strictly prohibited. DSLR requires prior permission. Email: dayvisits@iskcongev.com
  • Bookings: Only email communication is accepted for day visit bookings. WhatsApp/calls are not valid.
  • Spiritual Focus: GEV is a sacred ashram. Kindly engage in discussions about Lord Krishna.
  • Do not pluck flowers, fruits, or leaves.
  • Do not feed outside food to cows.
  • Keep the campus clean. Use dustbins. Spitting is not allowed.
  • Supervise children at all times.
  • Smoking, tobacco, alcohol, and gutka are strictly prohibited.
  • Outside food and drinks are not permitted.
  • Refrain from loud calls or playing film songs.
  • Do not feed the fish in water bodies.
  • Bathing in the Yamuna is not allowed.
  • Keep your belongings safe. The management is not responsible for losses.
