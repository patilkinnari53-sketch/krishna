About Govardhan Eco Village

Govardhan Eco Village (GEV), inspired by the teachings of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, is a sustainable spiritual community and retreat center located in the peaceful foothills of the Sahyadri mountains near Mumbai, Maharashtra.

It integrates ancient Vedic wisdom with modern sustainable living practices. The village houses a beautiful temple of Sri Sri Radha-Vrindavan Bihari, Goshala for cow protection, organic farming areas, traditional village cottages, and eco-friendly architecture that mirrors Vrindavan's sacred atmosphere.

Recognized globally for its environmental initiatives and spiritual ambience, GEV serves as a model for harmonious coexistence between nature and spirituality, attracting thousands of pilgrims, researchers, students, and seekers from across the world.

GEV Festival Highlights – 2025

  • Jan 10 – Putrada Ekadashi: Seek blessings for progeny.
  • Jan 25 – Shattila Ekadashi: Charity and offerings of sesame.
  • Feb 2 – Vasant Panchami: Saraswati Puja & spring welcome.
  • Mar 14 – Gaura Purnima: Appearance of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.
  • Apr 6 – Ram Navami: Appearance of Lord Ramachandra.
  • May 12 – Nrisimha Chaturdashi: Appearance of Lord Nrisimhadeva.
  • Jun 9 – Nirjala Ekadashi: The most austere and purifying Ekadashi.
  • Jul 10 – Guru Purnima / Vyasa Puja: Honoring spiritual masters.
  • Aug 16 – Krishna Janmashtami: Birth of Lord Sri Krishna.
  • Aug 31 – Radhastami: Appearance of Srimati Radharani.
  • Oct 21 – Govardhan Parikrama Begins: Sacred walk around Govardhan Hill.
  • Oct 22 – Govardhan Puja & Annakut: Mountain worship and grand prasadam offering.
  • Dec 1 – Mokshada Ekadashi & Gita Jayanti: Liberation-giving fast and the day Bhagavad Gita was spoken.
  • Dec 26 – Saphala Ekadashi: The Ekadashi of success and prosperity.
  • Dec 31 – Year-End Harinam & Fire Ceremony: Spiritual closure & purification.
